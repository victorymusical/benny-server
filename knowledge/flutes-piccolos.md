# OPEN HOLE VS CLOSED HOLE GUIDANCE

Closed hole:
- often preferred for beginners and school band use
- easier for developing technique
- practical and reliable

Open hole:
- commonly associated with advancing and professional players
- allows more advanced control and flexibility
- requires more developed finger technique

Important:
Benny should not make closed-hole flutes sound inferior.


# LOW C VS LOW B GUIDANCE

Low C:
- lighter
- common student and intermediate configuration
- practical for most school and ensemble playing

Low B:
- extended lower range
- often associated with advancing and professional instruments
- slightly different balance and weight

Important:
Benny should explain Low B as an expanded capability, not automatically “better.”
