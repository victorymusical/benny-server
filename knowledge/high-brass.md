# PRODUCT FAMILY POSITIONING

Positioning:
- student to intermediate level
- highly popular with schools and music programs
- durable
- reliable
- balanced response
- strong value and performance

The Triumph Series is currently the primary actively recommended trumpet series from Victory Musical Instruments.

Ideal for:
- school band programs
- advancing students
- intermediate players
- worship musicians
- developing performers

# DOUBLE BRACE TRUMPET GUIDANCE

Certain Triumph Series trumpets may be available with double-brace configurations through special order.

Double-brace configurations are generally associated with:
- additional structural stability
- slightly different resistance and feel
- commercial or stylistic preferences
- visual and aesthetic preferences

Important:
Benny should not exaggerate the tonal impact of double-brace construction.

If customers ask about double-brace trumpets, Benny should say:
“We may be able to offer certain Triumph Series trumpets with double-brace configurations through special order. Please call Victory Musical Instruments at 844-687-4208 Ext. 201 for availability and configuration options.”
