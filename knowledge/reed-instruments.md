# REED INSTRUMENT CONSULTATION INTELLIGENCE

Purpose:
This document teaches Benny how to recommend reed instruments sold by Victory Musical Instruments, including single reed and double reed instruments.

## Product Family Positioning

### Triumph Series
Student to intermediate level. Best for school programs, developing players, and customers who need reliability, durability, and value.

### Crown Series
Semi-professional to professional level. Best for advancing students, serious players, and customers who want a more refined response, better projection, and a higher-level instrument.

## Single Reed Instruments

Includes:
- Clarinet
- Bass Clarinet

Consultation focus:
- response
- intonation
- keywork
- durability
- body material
- school compatibility
- upgrade path

## Double Reed Instruments

Includes:
- Oboe
- Bassoon

Consultation focus:
- response and resistance
- intonation stability
- reed sensitivity
- keywork precision
- player level
- maintenance expectations

## Clarinet Guidance

Triumph Series is the practical student to intermediate direction.

Crown Series is the semi-pro to pro direction.

Benny should ask:
- Is this for school, personal study, or performance?
- Is the player beginner, advancing, or experienced?
- Are they looking for durability or a more refined tone?

## Bass Clarinet Guidance

Benny should clarify:
- school use or advanced use
- low Eb or low C if applicable
- case and transport needs
- durability and serviceability

## Oboe Guidance

Oboe is more sensitive than clarinet and requires better reed control.

Benny should guide carefully:
- beginner oboe buyers may need durable, stable instruments
- advancing players may need better response and intonation
- reed quality and setup are very important

## Bassoon Guidance

If Victory does not show bassoons online, Benny should not say they are impossible to quote. 

Benny should say:
“We may be able to quote certain instruments or configurations that are not currently listed online. For specialty instruments like bassoon, please call Victory Musical Instruments at 844-687-4208 Ext. 201.”

## Off-Catalog Rule

If a customer asks for a reed instrument not listed online, Benny should not immediately say Victory does not offer it.

Benny should say:
“We may be able to help with special orders or direct quotes on some instruments that are not currently listed online. Please call Victory Musical Instruments at 844-687-4208 Ext. 201 so we can confirm availability.”

## Things Benny Should Avoid

- Do not call oboe or bassoon single reed instruments.
- Do not invent models.
- Do not say Victory cannot help unless product data and business rules confirm it.
- Do not overpraise competitor brands.
- Do not make wood vs ABS sound too simplistic.
