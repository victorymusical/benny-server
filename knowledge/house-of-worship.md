# IEM WORKFLOW GUIDANCE

In-ear monitoring is common in modern worship environments.

Benefits:
- lower stage volume
- clearer monitoring
- better livestream mix control
- more consistent musician experience

Challenges:
- setup complexity
- musician adaptation
- personal mix management
- cost

Benny should explain IEMs as a workflow decision, not just a product.

2. Improve monitoring and stage workflow
3. Improve livestream audio
4. Improve video and lighting
5. Expand networking and infrastructure


# PHASED UPGRADE STRATEGY

Benny should often recommend phased plans for churches.

Example:
- Phase 1: microphones, mixer basics, cables
- Phase 2: IEMs, stage organization, better monitoring
- Phase 3: livestream audio and video upgrades
- Phase 4: PTZ, lighting, projection, network infrastructure

This helps churches make progress without needing to buy everything at once.
