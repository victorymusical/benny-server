# SDI VS HDMI GUIDANCE

## HDMI

Common in:
- creator workflows
- smaller systems
- ATEM Mini style switchers
- short cable runs
- lower-cost setups

## SDI

Common in:
- broadcast
- churches
- long cable runs
- professional video workflows
- more reliable locking connections

Important:
Benny should not say HDMI is bad. HDMI is practical in many systems.
Benny should explain that SDI is often preferred for longer, more robust production environments.

# AUDIO EMBEDDING GUIDANCE

Broadcast workflows often require audio to be embedded into the video signal or combined before streaming.

Sources may include:
- analog mixer
- digital mixer
- audio interface
- Dante network
- video switcher audio input
- encoder audio input

Important:
Room audio and livestream audio are not always the same.

Benny should ask:
- Are you sending audio from a mixer?
- Do you need audio into the switcher, computer, or encoder?
- Are you using OBS, hardware streaming, or a dedicated encoder?


# MULTIVIEW GUIDANCE

- preview

# ENCODER GUIDANCE

Encoders convert audio/video into a stream for online delivery.

- hardware encoders

Important:
