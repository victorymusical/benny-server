# OPEN-BACK VS CLOSED-BACK GUIDANCE

This is one of the most important distinctions in headphone consultation.


## Closed-Back Headphones

Topics:
- isolation
- tracking
- recording environments
- portability
- reduced microphone bleed

Ideal for:
- recording vocals
- recording instruments
- live tracking
- podcasting
- streaming
- mobile workflows

Important:
Closed-back headphones are often the most practical choice for recording workflows.


## Open-Back Headphones

Topics:
- wider soundstage
- natural listening
- mixing environments
- long listening sessions

Ideal for:
- mixing
- editing
- critical listening
- quieter studio environments

Important:
Open-back headphones leak sound and are usually not ideal for recording near microphones.


# TRACKING VS MIXING GUIDANCE

## Tracking Headphones

Topics:
- isolation
- comfort
- durability
- practical monitoring

Important:
Tracking headphones prioritize isolation and practical workflow.


## Mixing Headphones

Topics:
- detail
- stereo imaging
- accuracy
- tonal balance

Important:
Mixing headphones prioritize accuracy over exaggerated bass or consumer-style tuning.


# STUDIO MONITORS VS HEADPHONES GUIDANCE

## Studio Monitors

Topics:
- stereo imaging
- room interaction
- mixing accuracy
- fixed studio environments

Important:
Studio monitors perform best in reasonably controlled acoustic spaces.


## Headphones

Topics:
- portability
- untreated rooms
- tracking
- quiet environments

Important:
Headphones are often the better practical solution for:
- garage studios
- apartments
- untreated rooms
- mobile workflows


# UNTREATED ROOM GUIDANCE

Important:
Many customers record in untreated rooms.

Benny should understand:
- studio monitors may not perform optimally in highly reflective rooms
- headphones may sometimes provide more accurate practical monitoring
- untreated rooms affect bass response and stereo imaging

Important:
Benny should explain this naturally without scaring customers.
